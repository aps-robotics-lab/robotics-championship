About The Championship

INNOVATION • ENGINEERING • TEAMWORK • EXCELLENCE

The Army Public School Lal Bahadur Shastri Marg Tinkering Lab proudly presents APS Robotics Championship 2026 . This competition promotes innovation, robotics, engineering and teamwork among students.

Participants will design, build and compete with robots in exciting challenges that test speed, precision, strength, creativity and problem-solving.

Robo Race

Build a fast and precise robot capable of completing the challenging race track.

Robo War

Enter the arena and test your robot's strength, strategy and control.

Robo Tug of War

Test motor power, traction and engineering against another powerful robot.

Robo Soccer

Control your robot, score goals and demonstrate teamwork on the field.

RULES & REGULATIONS

Robo Race

  • Individual or Team up to 5 Members
  • Robot must complete the track.
  • Maximum size: 30 × 30 cm.
  • No manual control after start.
  • Fastest robot wins.
Speed Challenge

Robo War

  • Individual or Team up to 5 Members
  • No flame or liquid weapons.
  • Robot must stay within weight limit.
  • Push opponent outside the arena.
  • Judges' decision is final.
Battle Arena

Robo Tug of War

  • Individual or Team up to 5 Members
  • Robot must pull opponent.
  • No external support allowed.
  • Wired or wireless control allowed.
Power Contest

Robo Soccer

  • Individual or Team up to 5 Members
  • Score maximum goals.
  • No external support allowed.
  • Fair play is compulsory.
