WELCOME TO APS ROBOTICS CHAMPIONSHIP 2026

Welcome, Young Innovators.

Welcome to the APS Robotics Championship 2026, organised by the APS Tinkering Lab of Army Public School Lal Bahadur Shastri Marg, Lucknow. Bring your ideas, build with purpose, compete fairly and learn from every challenge.

01 / ABOUT

Where Ideas Become Machines

APS Robotics Championship 2026 is a hands-on robotics competition designed to encourage creativity, engineering, teamwork and problem solving.
