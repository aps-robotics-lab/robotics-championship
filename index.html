PHASE_04 // CORE LEADERSHIP & ADMINISTRATION

The command structure behind the arena.

Each department owns a critical part of the championship experience. Names can be assigned from the Registration Department without changing this interface.

CORE_01CHAMPIONSHIP

Event Manager

Owns the overall event schedule, arena flow, coordination and execution.

CORE_02ADMINISTRATION

Secretary

Maintains official records, communication, documentation and coordination.

CORE_03RACE ARENA

Robo Race Coordinator

Coordinates race setup, briefing, inspection and round execution.

CORE_04SAUCER ARENA

Robo Saucer Coordinator

Coordinates arena setup, match flow, scoring and event communication.

CORE_05TUG ARENA

Robot Tug of War Coordinator

Coordinates match pairing, arena readiness and competition procedure.

CORE_06WAR ARENA

Robo War Coordinator

Coordinates safety inspection, match flow, judging and arena discipline.

CORE_07LOGISTICS & OPERATIONS

Logistics Manager

Handles equipment movement, materials, schedules and operational readiness.

CORE_08VENUE

Venue Manager

Owns venue readiness, access, layouts, participant movement and facilities.

CORE_09OUTREACH

Marketing Head

Leads promotion, communication, media and championship outreach.

CORE_10DIGITAL SYSTEMS

Web Developer

Maintains the website, registration interface, digital workflows and web systems.

CORE_11ENGINEERING

Technical Head

Leads technical inspection, robot specifications, equipment and engineering support.

CORE_12PARTICIPANT SUPPORT

Helping Department

Handles participant questions, support tickets and championship assistance.

CORE_13OFFICIAL INTAKE

Registration Department

Reviews registrations, manages approval workflow and maintains participant records.
