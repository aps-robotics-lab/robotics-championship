ABOUT THE CHAMPIONSHIP

Engineering the Future

APS Robotics Championship 2026 is a student-focused robotics competition designed to transform creativity, engineering knowledge and teamwork into real-world robotic solutions.

Participants will design, build and operate robots while competing against other teams in exciting technical challenges.
