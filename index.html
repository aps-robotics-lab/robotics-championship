About The Championship

Innovation • Engineering • Teamwork • Excellence

The Army Public School Lal Bahadur Shastri Marg Tinkering Lab proudly presents the APS Robotics Championship 2026. This event provides young innovators with an opportunity to design, build, and compete using robotics while developing creativity, technical knowledge, and teamwork.

Participants will demonstrate problem-solving abilities through exciting robotics challenges designed to test speed, strength, precision, and control. Whether you're a beginner or an experienced robotics enthusiast, this competition is the perfect platform to showcase your talent.

Robo Race

Build a fast and accurate robot capable of completing the race track while overcoming obstacles in minimum time.

Robo War

Battle against opponents inside the arena using strategy, control, and engineering skills to dominate the competition.

Robo Tug of War

Design a powerful robot capable of pulling the opponent beyond the winning line while maintaining maximum traction.

Robo Soccer

Compete in an exciting football match where robots score goals through precision movement and teamwork.

Rules & Regulations

Robo Race

  • Individual or Team (5 Members)
  • Robot must complete the track.
  • Maximum size: 30 × 30 cm.
  • No manual control after start.
  • Fastest robot wins.
Speed Challenge

Robo War

  • Individual or Team of 5 members.
  • No flame or liquid weapons.
  • Robot must stay within weight limit.
  • Push opponent outside arena.
  • Judges' decision is final.
Battle Arena

Robo Tug of War

  • Individual or Team of 5 members.
  • Robot must pull opponent.
  • No external support allowed.
  • Wired or Wireless

Robo Socer

  • Individual or Team of 5 members.
  • Robot must pull opponent.
  • No external support allowed.
  • Wired or Wireless
