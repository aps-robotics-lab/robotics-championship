APS Robotics Championship 2026

The APS Robotics Championship is an exciting robotics competition presented by the Army Public School Lal Bahadur Shastri Marg Tinkering Lab.

Participants will design, build and operate robots while competing in challenges that test speed, precision, power, strategy, creativity and teamwork.

03 / EVENTS

CHOOSE YOUR ARENA

Four challenges. One championship.

01

Robo Race

SPEED CHALLENGE

Build a fast and precise robot capable of completing the challenging race track.

  • Robot must complete the track
  • Maximum size: 30 × 30 cm
  • No manual control after start
  • Fastest robot wins
02

Robo War

BATTLE ARENA

Test your robot's strength, strategy and control inside the battle arena.

  • Robot must stay within weight limit
  • No flame or liquid weapons
  • Push opponent outside arena
  • Judges' decision is final
03

Robo Tug of War

POWER CONTEST

Test motor power, traction and engineering against another powerful robot.

  • Robot must pull opponent
  • No external support
  • Wired or wireless control allowed
  • Engineering strength matters
04

Robo Soccer

TEAM GAME

Control your robot, score goals and demonstrate teamwork on the field.

  • Score maximum goals
  • No external support
  • Team coordination required
  • Fair play is compulsory
