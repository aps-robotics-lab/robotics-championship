THE CHAMPIONSHIP

Build. Think. Compete.

APS Robotics Championship 2026 is a technology-driven robotics competition designed to encourage creativity, engineering skills, teamwork and problem-solving.

Organized by the APS Tinkering Lab, the championship provides students with an exciting platform to transform ideas into working robotic systems.

Participants will design, build and operate robots while competing in challenging robotics arenas.
