About The Championship

Innovation • Engineering • Teamwork • Excellence

The Army Public School Lal Bahadur Shastri Marg Tinkering Lab proudly presents APS Robotics Championship 2026 . This competition aims to encourage innovation, robotics, engineering, creativity and teamwork among young innovators.

Participants will design, build and compete with robots in exciting challenges that test speed, precision, strength, strategy and problem-solving skills.

Robo Race

Build a fast and precise robot to complete the track.

Robo War

Put your robot's strength and strategy to the ultimate test.

Robo Tug of War

Test traction, power and engineering against another robot.

Robo Soccer

Control your robot and score goals against your opponents.

Competition Events

Rules & Regulations

Robo Race

  • Individual or Team (Up to 5 Members)
  • Robot must complete the track.
  • Maximum size: 30 × 30 cm.
  • No manual control after start.
  • Fastest robot wins.
Speed Challenge

Robo War

  • Individual or Team (Up to 5 Members)
  • No flame or liquid weapons.
  • Robot must stay within weight limit.
  • Push opponent outside arena.
  • Judges' decision is final.
Battle Arena

Robo Tug of War

  • Individual or Team (Up to 5 Members)
  • Robot must pull opponent.
  • No external support allowed.
  • Wired or Wireless allowed.
Power Contest

Robo Soccer

  • Individual or Team (Up to 5 Members)
  • Score maximum goals.
  • No external support allowed.
  • Fair play is compulsory.
