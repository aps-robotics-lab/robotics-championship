01 / ABOUT

Where Young Minds Build The Future

APS Robotics Championship 2026 is a platform for students to explore robotics, engineering, problem solving and teamwork through exciting hands-on competitions.

Organised through the APS Tinkering Lab at Army Public School, Lal Bahadur Shastri Marg, Lucknow, the championship encourages students to move beyond classroom learning and transform their ideas into working robotic systems.

Teams will compete in challenging robotic events designed to test speed, control, strategy, engineering and teamwork.
