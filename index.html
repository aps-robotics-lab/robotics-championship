ABOUT THE CHAMPIONSHIP

Where Ideas Become Machines

APS Robotics Championship 2026 is a technology-focused robotics competition organized by the APS Tinkering Lab.

The championship gives students an opportunity to transform their ideas into working robotic systems while developing engineering, programming, problem-solving and teamwork skills.

Participants can compete across multiple robotics challenges designed to encourage creativity, strategy and technical thinking.
