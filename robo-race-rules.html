ARENA_01 // SPEED & PRECISION

Robo Race Rules

Complete the marked course in the shortest valid time while maintaining control of the robot.

TECHNICAL INSPECTIONFAIR PLAYSAFETY PROTOCOL

1. Objective

  • Complete the designated course as quickly as possible while maintaining valid control.
  • The fastest valid run after applicable penalties determines the result.

2. Arena & Movement

  • The robot must remain within the designated track or arena.
  • Leaving the course may result in a penalty or restart according to the judge.
  • Intentional collision with barriers or interference with another competitor is not permitted.

3. Control & Start

  • Wired or wireless control may be used subject to the arena technical requirements.
  • The robot may begin only on the judge’s signal.
  • False starts, unsafe operation or repeated controller failure may result in a restart or time penalty.

4. Safety

  • Every robot must pass technical inspection before entering the arena.
  • Secure wiring, insulated connections and a safe power-disconnect method are required where applicable.
  • Judges may stop an unsafe robot immediately.
IMPORTANT: Event-day briefing, technical inspection and judge instructions may provide additional arena-specific requirements. Those safety instructions take precedence where stricter.
