General Rules Eligibility Technical Events Safety Conduct
01 / GENERAL

General Rules

Rules applicable to all participating teams and competition categories.

01

Team Registration

Every team must complete the official registration process before the registration deadline.

02

Team Identity

Each team must have a unique team name and should provide accurate participant information during registration.

03

Reporting Time

Teams must report to the venue at the time specified by the event organizers. Late arrival may affect participation.

04

Organizer Authority

The decision of the judges and event coordinators regarding competition matters shall be considered final.

05

Rule Changes

Organizers reserve the right to modify technical or competition rules when required for safety or fair competition.

02 / ELIGIBILITY

Eligibility Rules

Participation requirements for teams and students.

Students

Participants must be eligible students according to the competition guidelines issued by the organizers.

Team Members

Team size must follow the limits announced for the selected competition category.

Identification

Participants may be required to present valid school identification when requested.

Verification

Organizers may verify registration and participant information before participation.

03 / TECHNICAL

Technical Regulations

Important technical requirements for robots.

Robot Dimensions

Robot dimensions and weight must remain within the limits announced for the respective competition category.

Power Supply

Only permitted battery and power systems may be used. Unsafe or damaged batteries are not permitted.

Mechanical Construction

Robots must be constructed securely. Loose components that may create a safety hazard are not permitted.

Wireless Control

Wireless systems must operate using permitted communication equipment and frequencies.

Inspection

All robots may be inspected before the competition. Robots failing inspection may require modification before competing.

04 / COMPETITIONS

Event Rules

Competition-specific information.

EVENT 01

Robo Race

01

Robots must complete the designated race track as quickly as possible.

02

Robots must remain within the designated track boundaries.

03

Deliberately obstructing another robot may result in penalties.

04

The winner will be determined according to the official timing and scoring system.

EVENT 02

Robo War

01

Robots compete inside the designated arena according to the match format.

02

Robots must remain within the permitted weight and dimensional limits.

03

Dangerous mechanisms that may cause uncontrolled injury or damage are prohibited.

04

Judges may stop a match if a robot becomes unsafe or violates competition regulations.

EVENT 03

Robo Tug of War

01

Two robots compete against each other using the designated pulling mechanism.

02

Robots must begin from their assigned starting positions.

03

The robot successfully pulling its opponent beyond the designated line wins.

04

Any unsafe interference with the opponent may result in disqualification.

EVENT 04

Robo Soccer

01

Teams compete to score goals using their robotic players.

02

Robots must remain within the permitted dimensions and weight.

03

Deliberate damage to another team's robot is prohibited.

04

The team with the higher official score at the end of the match wins.

05 / SAFETY

Safety Regulations

Safety takes priority over competition.

No Dangerous Components

Components that create unreasonable danger to participants, judges or spectators are prohibited.

Battery Safety

Damaged, leaking or unsafe batteries must not be used during the competition.

Safe Operation

Robots must be operated responsibly and only inside designated competition areas.

Immediate Stop

Judges may stop any robot or match whenever they identify a safety risk.

06 / CONDUCT

Participant Conduct

Fair play and respectful behaviour are expected from every participant.

Respect

Participants must respect other teams, judges, volunteers and organizers.

Fair Play

Cheating, deliberate interference and manipulation of competition equipment are prohibited.

Disqualification

Serious violations of the rules may result in penalties or disqualification.

Important Notice

Participants are responsible for reading and understanding the applicable rules before the competition. The organizers and judges reserve the right to interpret and enforce the rules to ensure safety and fair competition.
