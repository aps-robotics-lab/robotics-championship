COMPETITION HANDBOOK

Championship Rules

Review the competition framework, safety requirements and robot specifications before registering your team.

01

General Rules

  • Participants must follow instructions from the organising team and judges.
  • Teams must report on time for verification and competition rounds.
  • Unsafe behaviour or deliberate damage may lead to disqualification.
02

Team Rules

  • Teams must use the participant and team details submitted during registration.
  • All members must comply with the event eligibility requirements and school instructions.
  • Each team is responsible for its robot, equipment and personal belongings.
03

Robot Safety

  • All robots must undergo technical inspection before participation.
  • Hazardous modifications, unsafe components or equipment that may endanger participants are strictly prohibited.
  • Judges or organisers may stop a robot at any time if it creates a safety concern.
04

Event Conduct

  • Participants must follow all event-specific arena and competition instructions.
  • Judge decisions during a round shall be considered final unless an official appeal process is announced by the organisers.
  • Participants must maintain respectful conduct towards opponents, volunteers, judges and competition equipment.
05

Robot Technical Specifications

All participating robots must comply with the following technical specifications for their respective events.

⚙

General Technical Requirements

All participating robots must comply with the prescribed weight, dimensional and voltage limits for their respective events. Robots exceeding the specified limits may be subject to further inspection and may be disqualified at the discretion of the event organisers.

Participants are advised to verify their robot's technical specifications before reporting for the competition.

IMPORTANT NOTICE

Competition Requirements

All participants are expected to familiarise themselves with the competition rules, safety requirements and technical specifications before arriving at the championship venue.

The organising team reserves the right to inspect participating robots and take appropriate action against any robot or team that does not comply with the prescribed requirements.
