COMPETITION HANDBOOK

Championship Rules

Review the competition framework before registering your team.

01

General Rules

  • Participants must follow instructions from the organising team and judges.
  • Teams must report on time for verification and competition rounds.
  • Unsafe behaviour or deliberate damage may lead to disqualification.
02

Team Rules

  • Use the team details entered during registration.
  • All members must follow the event eligibility and school instructions.
  • Teams are responsible for their robot and equipment.
03

Robot Safety

  • Robots must be inspected before participation.
  • Hazardous modifications or unsafe components are not permitted.
  • Judges may stop a robot if it creates a safety concern.
04

Event Conduct

  • Follow event-specific arena instructions.
  • Judge decisions during a round are final unless the organisers announce an appeal process.
  • Respect opponents, volunteers and equipment.

Important

Event-specific dimensions, weight limits, scoring and technical specifications may be announced by the organising team. Check official championship instructions before the event.
